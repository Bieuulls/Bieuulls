## 🇺🇸 English Version

### 🌌 About Me

I am a **Graphic Designer**, **Science Fiction Author**, and a passionate researcher pushing the boundaries of **Generative Artificial Intelligence**.

My work is driven by a constant quest to elevate visual craftsmanship and amplify creative workflows. I thrive at the intersection of mathematical precision (Bézier curves & vectors), immersive speculative fiction, and cutting-edge autonomous AI tools.

* 🎨 **Visual & Vector Design:** Specialized in branding, brand identity, geometric precision, pixel-perfect iconography, and refined typography.
* 🤖 **AI Exploration & Automation:** Active research into generative models, context engineering, open protocols (**Model Context Protocol - MCP**), and ExtendScript automations that eliminate repetitive tasks.
* ✍️ **Literary Creation:** Author and architect of dystopian and psychological cyberpunk worlds exploring how technology reshapes human consciousness.
* 🌐 **Open Source Mindset:** A devoted admirer of GitHub and the global developer community building the open-source infrastructure of our future.

### ⚡ Featured Project: [Illustrator-AI-MCP](https://github.com/Bieuulls/Illustrator-AI-MCP)

*A high-speed bridge connecting Adobe Illustrator with advanced AI models through the Model Context Protocol (MCP).*

* 💎 **Native Dark UI:** CEP panel matching Adobe Illustrator’s native palette with zero visual friction.
* ⚡ **Model Context Protocol (MCP):** Connects directly with Claude Desktop and autonomous AI agents to manipulate vector shapes in real time.
* 🛠️ **ExtendScript Engine:** Direct artboard, layer, and path execution with zero latency.
* 📦 **1-Click Automated Installers:** Single-command setup for both Windows and macOS with community contribution guides.

### 🌌 Sobre Mim

Sou **Designer Gráfico**, **Autor de Ficção Científica** e pesquisador das fronteiras da **Inteligência Artificial**. 

Minha jornada profissional é focada em refinar a criação visual e expandir a produtividade criativa ao seu nível máximo. Busco a harmonia entre o rigor matemático das curvas Bézier, a profundidade narrativa da literatura especulativa e as possibilidades dos modelos generativos de IA.

* 🎨 **Design Visual & Vetorial:** Identidades visuais memoráveis, geometria precisa, iconografia milimétrica e composição tipográfica.
* 🤖 **Exploração de IA & Automação:** Investigação ativa de modelos generativos, engenharia de contexto, protocolos abertos (**MCP**) e automações para eliminar tarefas manuais e liberar foco para o processo criativo.
* ✍️ **Criação Literária:** Autor da saga cyberpunk e psicológica *Fracture Experiment*, investigando a consciência humana e a evolução tecnológica.
* 🌐 **Cultura Open Source:** Entusiasta do GitHub e de todos os engenheiros, designers e inventores que compartilham código aberto para construir o futuro.
